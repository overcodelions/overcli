# Trademark policy

**Overcli**, the Overcli name, the Overcodelions name, and the project logos and
icons are trademarks of Lionel Farr and Owen Farr (unregistered, "™").

The [Apache-2.0 license](LICENSE) covers the **code**. It does not grant any
right to use the project's *name* or *logo*. This policy explains what we ask,
and it is deliberately permissive — the goal is only that someone who downloads
something called "Overcli" gets the thing we published.

## You do not need to ask

- Use the name to refer to this project truthfully: "works with Overcli",
  "a plugin for Overcli", "built on Overcli", "I use Overcli".
- Write about it, review it, teach it, screenshot it, link to it.
- Redistribute the **unmodified** official builds under the name Overcli,
  including in a package manager, as long as you link back to https://overcli.app.
- Fork the repository and say your fork is "a fork of Overcli".

## Please rename first

If you distribute a **modified** version to other people, give it your own name
and your own icon. Don't call the binary Overcli, don't ship it under the
Overcli icon, and don't use https://overcli.app or a confusingly similar domain.
Say "based on Overcli" instead — that's accurate and it's welcome.

## Never

- Implying the project endorses, sponsors, or maintains your version when it
  doesn't.
- Registering the name, a close variant, or the logo as your own mark, domain,
  package, app-store listing, or social account.

## The official source

The only official builds come from
[github.com/overcodelions/overcli/releases](https://overcli.app) and are linked from
[https://overcli.app](https://overcli.app). Anything else is someone else's build, however
it is labelled.

## Questions

Ask. Open an issue, or reach the maintainers via [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if
it's sensitive. Permission is usually a short conversation, and we would rather
say yes than find out later.
